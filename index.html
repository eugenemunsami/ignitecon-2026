The Invitation

THIS IS NOT ANOTHER WORKSHOP. IT IS FOUR DAYS OF FIRE — HARD TRUTHS, SHARPENED SKILLS, REAL BUYERS, AND THE FOUNDERS WHO ARE DONE PLAYING SMALL.

The Arc

INWARD. OUTWARD. INTO THE FIRE.

Four days engineered as chapters. Each one builds on the last. Each one earns its place.

CH. 01
Wednesday

THE AWAKENING

Meet the version of yourself running the show without your permission. Confront the money story, the failure story, the worthiness story.

CH. 02
Thursday

THE KINDLING

Take aim. Build the fire. Marketing, sales, product — sharpened in the field first, unpacked in the workshop after.

CH. 03
Friday

THE BLAZE

The deep end. Money. Compliance. Risk. Amazing Race in the morning, the Flame Awards on the deck under the stars.

CH. 04
Saturday

FIRE IN THE ROOM

Buyers. Funders. Procurement. Your pitch plays over breakfast. You shake the hands that open the doors.
